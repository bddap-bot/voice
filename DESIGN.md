# Voice stage

The set is a shallow room viewed straight on. A warm back wall fills the frame and two darker side walls converge toward it. The puppet stands in the visual centre, drawn by a fixed camera, so the figure's feet always sit at 89% of the canvas height and the strip below them is the set's floor.

Above 720 CSS pixels wide, and on any screen 540 CSS pixels tall or less, the stage is three columns: the delegation log on the left wall, the puppet in the middle, and the display, which the figure points at, on the right wall. A dotted grip on the inner edge of each wall panel resizes it by drag, arrow keys, or double-click to reset; the puppet's column takes the remaining width, and a panel grows until the puppet's column is 200 CSS pixels wide, enough for the minimized card. The canvas is centred on that column and extends behind the panels to the outer edge of the narrower one, so a raised or pointing arm is never cut by the canvas edge while there is room beside it; the panels and grips sit in front of it. A thin status line with the session time sits above the canvas, and the canvas ends above a desk strip that holds the credit and the controls card.

In portrait at 720 CSS pixels wide or below, the stage stacks: status line, puppet, display, delegation log, and a dock row for the controls card. A grip above each panel resizes its height, down to 80 CSS pixels and up until the puppet row is 120 CSS pixels tall. Panel sizes are kept per layout in local storage.

Full screen is the top of the same resize: pulling a grip past the panel's largest size, pressing its grow arrow at that size, or pressing Full screen in the panel's header makes the panel cover the page, with the minimized card still in front of it. Exit full screen in the header, Escape, the shrink arrow, or dragging back before letting go returns it to its size; the status line hides while a panel is full. Full screen is not saved.

All controls live on one card. Minimized, it shows only Mute mic and the expand button. Expanded, it groups Microphone, Appearance, Send to the hub, and Device: two columns on wide screens and a one-column sheet on phones, every button at least 44 CSS pixels tall. Before a token is saved, the card opens on its own to the token entry.

The composition should answer two questions in every rendered frame: does anything sit directly behind the puppet, and does the whole page read as a stage? Headless Chromium projects the feet through the runtime camera and compares the rendered rectangles of the status line, credit, minimized card, panels and grips with the safe rectangle and with each other at phone, landscape, laptop, and TV sizes; every intersection count must be zero, and on laptop and TV the canvas must end above the card and be wider than both panels. The stage reading comes from one continuous room, converging walls, pools of light, and functional surfaces that belong to the set.
