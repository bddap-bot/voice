# Voice stage

The set is a shallow room viewed straight on. A warm back wall fills the frame, two darker side walls converge toward it, and a low plinth holds the puppet in the visual centre. At widths above 720 CSS pixels, the puppet safe rectangle is 32% of the stage width by 68% of its visual-viewport height, positioned 34% from the left and 5% from the top. At phone widths it is 90% wide, positioned 5% from the left and 3% from the top, and ends at the first visual viewport. This conservative rectangle contains the whole projected performer throughout animation.

On laptop and TV, the delegation log is a panel on the left wall and the display is the brighter panel on the right, matching the performer runtime's rightward panel gaze. Authentication occupies the upper-left edge because that zone is outside both the puppet and the log. Appearance, credit, and session time are engraved into the plinth below the puppet. Text and image input forms a low desk across the foreground. The side panels end before the safe rectangle begins, and the plinth labels and desk begin below it.

At 720 CSS pixels wide or below, and on short coarse-pointer landscape screens, the first visual viewport is the uninterrupted puppet stage. The set continues below the fold into a compact control room: status and authentication, plinth details, input, display, and log appear in that order. The stage height is read from `visualViewport.height` at load and on each visual-viewport resize. Canvas drawing-buffer changes and elapsed time therefore cannot feed back into layout height.

The composition should answer two questions in every rendered frame: does anything sit directly behind the puppet, and does the whole page read as a stage? Headless Chromium compares the rendered rectangles of status, authentication, appearance, credit, timer, input, display, and log with the conservative safe rectangle at phone, laptop, and TV sizes; every intersection count must be zero. The stage reading comes from one continuous room, converging walls, pools of light, a central plinth, and functional surfaces that belong to the set.
